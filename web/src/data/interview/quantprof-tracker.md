## How to sync

- `#` is the problem's position in QuantProf's default problem list (`/problems`, no filters). New problems are added at the end, so anything past the last row here is new.
- Titles are unique on QuantProf; match on the title.
- ✅ = in the bank, with the matching item id(s). Several ids means the bank has variants of the same problem.
- Older databank `source` fields sometimes say only "Easy Probability", "IG", or a bare number. A bare number is the problem's `#` in this list when it was added. Those items were matched by comparing problem text.
