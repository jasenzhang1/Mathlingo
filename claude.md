## 1 Lesson Page

The fundamental building block of Mathlingo is the lesson. Once the customer gets proficient at the lesson, they are allowed to move onto the next lesson(s).

#### 1.1 Slides

Each lesson page has a quick slidedeck that is created in canva. This serves as the starting point of one's learning on the topic. These slides are man-made.

#### 1.2 Wiki

This page is most similar to a textbook. It gives the formal definitions and has some basic examples to build familiarity with using the concept.

#### 1.3 Conversational Learning

This serves as a "discussion section" for the user. They engage in dialog with their AI instructor, where the student may ask for practice questions, ask for analogies or more nuanced ways to enrich their understanding, or engage in other back-and-forth methods of learning. 

The user may choose to highlight and keep certain output from their interaction here so that they can come back and remember about the notes they took.


#### 1.4 Assessment

This final step checks for understanding. The student will be assessed in multiple ways. First, typical definitions and exact answer questions. Then, open-ended questions about a method and why it works. And finally, a multidisciplinary question that may allude to previous concepts that they should remember. 

Depending on the speed and accuracy of their responses, the AI grader will give them a grade and grades from previous units may also be affected.
